# StockTrack - iPhone Service Center Management & Customer CRM

A modern, premium web application for managing iPhone repairs, customers, technicians, and service operations.

## Features

- **Customer Management**: Complete customer database with contact info and service history
- **Service Center Entry**: Detailed device intake forms with photos and condition assessment
- **Repair Job Tracking**: Visual repair status tracking from receipt to delivery
- **IMEI Tracking**: Powerful search and tracking by IMEI, phone number, customer name, or Job ID
- **Technician Management**: Assign jobs, track workload and completion rates
- **Calling CRM**: Lead management and customer calling tracking
- **Follow-up Management**: Automated follow-up scheduling and reminders
- **Inventory Management**: Spare parts tracking with low-stock alerts
- **Payment & Invoice**: Professional invoicing and payment tracking
- **Advanced Search**: Global search across customers, devices, and jobs
- **Role-Based Access**: Admin, Manager, Technician, and Calling Staff roles
- **Notifications**: Real-time alerts for follow-ups, completions, and inventory

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Authentication**: JWT
- **UI Components**: Custom components + Heroicons
